CrackDetect

Description: A multimodal deep learning approach that combines visual and acoustic information to detect and classify building cracks more accurately and robustly than image-only methods.

Features

  • Multimodal fusion of image (ResNet50) and sound (1D ResNet) features
  • Attention-based feature fusion
  • 3-class severity classification: No Crack, Minor Crack, Major Crack
  • Trained on 12,000+ paired image and audio samples across 250+ buildings
  • 95.6% accuracy, 0.971 AUC-ROC, ~38ms inference time per sample
  • Robust to noise, lighting variation, and complex backgrounds
  • Lightweight enough for real-time, edge-device deployment

Workflow

Data Collection→Preprocessing→Feature Extraction→Attention Fusion→Crack Prediction
