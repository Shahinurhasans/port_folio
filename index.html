Rx-Guard: AI & Blockchain Antimicrobial Stewardship

Multimodal Building Crack Detection

Description: A multimodal deep learning system for structural crack detection in concrete, fusing visual features from a fine-tuned ResNet50 with acoustic features extracted via a mel-spectrogram CNN using a gated attention fusion mechanism.

Features

  • Fine-tuned ResNet50 visual feature extractor paired with mel-spectrogram CNN acoustic encoder
  • Gated attention fusion mechanism weighting visual and acoustic modalities adaptively
  • 3-class severity classification: No Crack, Minor Crack, Major Crack
  • Robust to lighting variations, surface texture noise, and complex real-world backgrounds

Workflow

Image + Audio Input→ResNet50 & Mel-CNN→Gated Attention Fusion→Crack Classification
